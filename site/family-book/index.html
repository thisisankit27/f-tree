Home/Family book

A book of your family, made to send

Turn your tree into a printable PDF: a cover, everyone in the family, a generation at a time, and an index to find yourself in. Made on your own device.

Choose a template

Each one is a different way to lay out the same family. Pick one and the page beside it changes.

Template to preview

Downloaded templates are checked against a signed catalogue before they are used, and you can remove a copy any time to free the space.

One tap from tree to PDF

The family book is a designed document you can print, send on WhatsApp or keep. It is made on your phone or laptop from the tree you already have. Nothing is uploaded to make it.

Living people are protected

They show only a birth year unless you turn full dates on, and notes are left out unless you include them.

Choose whose story it is

The book can be told around one person, and Hindi titles print correctly because Devanagari is set with its own typefaces.

It tells you the size first

A file budget keeps the PDF easy to send, and the estimate is shown before you make it.
